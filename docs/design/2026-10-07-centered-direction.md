# Centered direction — “Boşver, yıkat.”

User corrections supersede the first pass: center the text, make motion visible at arrival, improve the copy substantially, and ideate, reason and plan each section before implementation. The user's headline direction is “Boşver, yıkat.”

## Latest copy correction

The user rejected punctuation-separated fragments as vague branding. The hero remains “Boşver, yıkat” without a final period. All supporting language must read as natural Turkish and name the real service or action. No headline made of a sequence of isolated words or repeated sentence fragments.

## Whole-site decisions

- Voice: short, colloquial, assured. Physical chores and recognizable moments; no generic promises about living life. Retain the requested colloquial “Boşver” spelling as brand copy.
- Main headings, supporting copy and action groups are centered. Forms retain familiar input alignment for usability.
- The original app illustrations are the material, but staging is designed for a website. One central idea in each scene.
- There must be visible motion without scrolling: slow bobbing and rotation of objects, moving soap droplets, an orchestrated cleaning entrance. In-view playback and a persistent pause control prevent motion fatigue. Reduced motion stops the scene and retains every action.
- White, original blue and readable deep blue; Bricolage Grotesque; wide content frame. No invented prices, testimonials, earnings, or customer counts.
- Exactly three navigation destinations. WhatsApp, download and directions remain the three customer actions.
- All work stays on localhost. Partner applications are preview receipts, not live business submissions.

## Home 1 — Arrival

**Ideation:** A revolving washing machine, a video background, and a chore constellation were considered. The machine narrows the brand to laundry and video hides the app's identity. Choose a constellation of the actual service objects framing a centered statement.

**Reasoning:** “Boşver, yıkat.” does the emotional work. Motion must be obvious immediately, but leave the words stable and readable. The objects feel like a chore leaving your hands.

**Copy:** “Boşver, yıkat” / “Temizliğe ayıracağın vakit sana kalsın. Yıkatacaklarını seç, kapından alalım.” / three action buttons.

**Plan:** Centered display text, three centered CTAs, generous sky around them. A shirt floats on the left, laundry bag on the right, shoes below. The app blue forms a soft basin of light, not a rectangular card. Foam sweeps out at entry; each object rises and rotates on a different 6–9 second rhythm. A few bubbles travel upward and disappear. Pointer movement adds a small response; it is supplementary to autonomous movement. Mobile puts the constellation beneath the centered copy. Always provide pause and replay.

## Home 2 — What to hand off

**Ideation:** Five feature tiles, an accordion, and a service carousel were considered. Choose a single central service stage with a strip of five real objects as the control.

**Reasoning:** Visitors recognize the chore before reading the category. Each selection needs a distinct line of copy, not five variations on “professional cleaning.”

**Copy per choice:** Ayakkabı: “O çiftin daha çok yolu var.” Çamaşır: “Sepet dolduysa, boşver.” Ütü: “Ütü bekliyorsa, boşver.” Kuru temizleme: “O ceketi özel güne saklama.” Yorgan: “Makineye sığmadı diye aklında kalmasın.” Section introduction: “Bugün neyi boşveriyoruz?”

**Plan:** Five accessible, keyboard-operated service selectors above a centered object on an open stage. Selecting a service brings its figure forward with a short turn and soft ripple; its specific copy sits centered below. Continuous small object movement is visible while the section is in view. Availability caveat remains explicit. No uniform card grid.

## Home 3 — Hand-off journey

**Ideation:** Three independent feature cards versus one bag moving through three locations. Choose the moving bag.

**Reasoning:** Movement should explain the service, not decorate a list. The visitor follows the same item from door to care to return.

**Copy:** “Temizliğin kapından başlasın” Steps: “Sen hazırla.” / “Biz temizleyelim.” / “Tertemiz dönsün.” Supporting copy explains choosing a pickup slot, material-specific care and delivery.

**Plan:** A wide visual route with three stops and one blue bag. It moves to each stop; a short foam sweep appears at care; a check appears at return. Three step buttons let the visitor select directly. Auto-advance occurs only in view, stops after a manual selection, and obeys global pause. Supporting text is centered beneath the journey. At mobile widths retain the horizontal route with smaller, readable stops rather than shrinking a desktop diagram.

## Home 4 — Reassurance in the app

**Ideation:** Side-by-side marketing copy and phone, three phone cards, or a single centered phone stage. Choose the single central phone with a horizontal control strip.

**Reasoning:** The site should communicate one app capability at a time. Status transitions provide meaningful movement and reduce cognitive load.

**Copy:** “Siparişin aklında kalmasın” / “Alım saatini seç. Siparişinin durumunu gör. Bir dahaki sefere, favorinden tekrar yıkat.”

**Plan:** Deep blue full-width scene. Centered heading and introduction, three horizontally arranged feature controls, central phone. The selected screen visibly changes and the phone gently settles at a new angle. Brief notification tags are tied to the selected feature, not random simulated activity. Auto-cycle only in view and stop after interaction. App Store and Android status below the centered download CTA.

## Home 5 — Physical reassurance and action

**Ideation:** A small side map versus a wide location scene. Choose the wide map with a central address card and centered copy above.

**Reasoning:** The shop is a real place that makes the service tangible. This section should be useful and restful after the app scene.

**Copy:** “Yolun Bakırköy’e düşerse bekleriz” / “Ayakkabılarını elden bırakmak istersen Bakırköy’deyiz. Aklına takılan bir şey varsa, bir mesaj yeter.”

**Plan:** Centered headline, short paragraph, wide real map. Address and opening hours sit together below the map; WhatsApp/download/directions close the page. Map motion is a single small settling movement of the address marker on arrival and a hover response, never a moving map. Retain attribution and native map destinations.

## Product 1 — Product entrance

**Ideation:** A screenshot gallery versus an app opening into a real journey. Choose a centered hero with a layered phone and two small service objects.

**Reasoning:** The user arrives here to understand the app. The interface must dominate, with text giving a simple reason to use it.

**Copy:** “Yıkatmak birkaç dokunuş” / “Yıkatacaklarını, adresini ve sana uyan zamanı seçerek siparişini oluştur.”

**Plan:** Centered copy and app download above a gently moving phone. Side objects frame the device; avoid another copy-heavy split hero. iOS live and Android soon are explicit. Motion begins immediately and pauses with the site control.

## Product 2 — Three decisions

**Ideation:** Repeat the homepage app tabs or let the visitor try the choices. Choose a contained interactive walkthrough.

**Reasoning:** Actual decisions teach more than a list of benefits. Keep it visibly an example and never imply an order was placed.

**Copy:** “Sana uyan siparişi birlikte hazırlayalım” / “Hizmetini, adresini ve alım saatini seçmenin ne kadar kolay olduğunu keşfet.”

**Plan:** Three step buttons above one centered demo canvas. Service choice swaps the object; address choice changes a small door scene; time choice highlights a slot. A short summary updates in place. No real addresses, price promises or checkout. The single download action follows the example. Selection animation is fast and interruptible.

## Product 3 — Follow the care

**Ideation:** Another phone image versus a tracking strip. Choose a full-width timeline with a central live-feeling status illustration.

**Reasoning:** Tracking is a separate benefit from creating an order. Give it its own visual grammar.

**Copy:** “Nerede kaldığını merak etme.” / “Alındı mı, temizleniyor mu, hazır mı? Siparişinin adımlarını uygulamada gör.”

**Plan:** A single large current-status object and four status labels. Controlled progression moves a small indicator along the line. No fictitious customer or delivery time. Frame as an example of the app. Center all supporting copy.

## Product 4 — Services and questions

**Ideation:** Repeat the large homepage selector or provide direct service rows and practical questions. Choose a compact row of original service illustrations, then centered FAQ.

**Reasoning:** The product page should add depth without repeating the entire homepage. Service availability, payments and Android timing are the remaining decisions.

**Copy:** “Her parçanın ihtiyacı başka.” / “Başlamadan önce.”

**Plan:** A horizontal service illustration strip with plain labels and an address-availability note. Each illustration responds on hover/focus. FAQ uses native disclosure elements with centered questions and readable centered answers. Finish with centered three-action CTA.

## Partners 1 — Skilled work, more doors

**Ideation:** Revenue counters, an abstract network, or an example order moving through a workshop panel. Choose the panel and actual service objects; no earnings promise.

**Reasoning:** The business owner should recognize their work and see operational order. The animation must communicate a job changing stage.

**Copy:** “Özenin, daha çok kapıya ulaşsın.” / “İşini iyi yapıyorsan tanışalım. Verdiğin hizmeti, bölgeni ve birlikte nasıl çalışabileceğimizi konuşalım.”

**Plan:** Centered headline and application CTA. A wide operations scene below shows a highlighted sample job progressing between Yeni / Temizlikte / Hazır. Original garment art frames the panel. The scene is labeled as an example. Progress pauses after a short cycle or with the site motion control.

## Partners 2 — Partnership, concretely

**Ideation:** Three promotional cards versus a connected operational flow. Choose a connected customer / workshop / delivery story.

**Reasoning:** The business needs to know what the collaboration changes: customer reach, visibility over work, and agreed capacity. Explain these without suggesting guaranteed volume.

**Copy:** “İşine odaklanabileceğin bir çalışma düzeni” / “Yeni müşterilerle tanışırken siparişlerini ve iş aşamalarını aynı panelden takip et.”

**Plan:** Centered heading and three spaced, connected stages on a single wide surface. A small service object advances through the flow. Each stage has a distinct illustration, short title and concrete description. Commercial conditions remain subject to discussion.

## Partners 3 — Application

**Ideation:** Side-by-side sales copy and long form versus a direct centered invitation. Choose the centered invitation and compact form.

**Reasoning:** By this point, the visitor needs to act. Remove competing content and make the field order obvious.

**Copy:** “Önce tanışalım.” / “İşletmeni kısaca anlat. Birlikte neler yapabileceğimize bakalım.”

**Plan:** Three short review steps above a centered form, maximum 650px. Input labels remain readable, errors stay with the form, success replaces the form and receives focus. Local preview disclosure remains visible. No decorative movement while typing; only a short confirmation transition.

## Partners 4 — Open questions

**Ideation:** Long policy copy versus concise disclosures. Choose a restrained centered FAQ.

**Reasoning:** Eligibility, commercial conditions and acceptance need honest answers, not sales friction.

**Copy:** “Aklında bir soru kaldıysa.”

**Plan:** Centered FAQ with four concrete questions, followed by a direct WhatsApp contact. Native keyboard operation; icon rotation only on deliberate opening. This calm ending balances the living introduction.

## Acceptance before handoff

Visually inspect all three routes at desktop and mobile, verify motion is clearly visible at rest, pause/reduced motion stop it, text blocks are centered, CTA targets are correct, native dialogs restore focus, local form saves with explicit preview confirmation, invalid and duplicate requests are handled, and no changes are deployed.
