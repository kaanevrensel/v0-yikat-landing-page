# Living water and a broader YIKAT component language

Local preview only. This supersedes the previous finite wash / pointer-only
background. The owner explicitly wants autonomous, unmistakable motion and more
carefully designed components, while keeping the accepted blue, tactile identity
and minimal Turkish copy.

The physical reference is the app's blue shoe box, soft garment bag and four-point
care mark: rounded manufactured objects, precise seams, white highlights and
blue depth. Keep Bricolage Grotesque, original service art and the glass navbar.
Palette: ink #123b5b, action #1a68c1, YIKAT #269aff, water #dceeff, white.
Jhey supplies the flowing-water expression, Jakub the finishing and scale,
Emil the immediate controls. No stock icon grid or new marketing paragraphs.

## Composition

- Home: three continuously deforming water currents, reflective edges and sparse
  soap lenses. Motion starts without hovering and remains visible on touch.
  Decorative canvas has a static SVG fallback; pause work offscreen/hidden and
  honor reduced motion. Keep readable choices and all five within the first view.
- A small original icon family: conversation, place, app, collection, cleaning,
  ready and time. Shared blue material, distinct silhouettes, no generic icon set.
- Service selection: a perforated care ticket; art above a clear set of action
  rows, with icon, concise label and arrow. Preserve native-dialog behavior.
- Shop: an illustrated storefront with a folded real-map treatment, composed as
  a destination rather than another two-column white card. Keep real directions.
- Product: four distinct care-stage illustrations and an actual selected-time
  dial. The interactive example stays an example; no backend changes.
- Partners: an asymmetric map / workboard / calendar composition replaces the
  repeated number-and-paragraph benefits. Keep the approved hero and Başvur form.
