# Editorial reset after owner review

This supersedes the decorative parts of the previous centered direction. The owner rejected the pause icon, eyebrow copy, assurance checklists, repeated platform labels, forced headlines, generic icons and basic layouts. They explicitly prefer the previous Apple-like liquid-glass navigation. Retain the centered type, original brand, three routes and localhost boundary.

## Home

1. **Arrival.** One garment makes the service tangible and gives the visitor something meaningful to control. Build it directly in React Three Fiber: sampled fabric geometry, collar and hem, procedural weave and stains, studio lighting, soap bubbles and a movable wash front. Use one oversized centered headline, one explanatory sentence and three plain actions. Pointer movement changes the viewing angle; dragging or the native keyboard range cleans the garment. The introduction finishes in 4.2 seconds, then leaves control to the visitor. React/SVG supplies the WebGL fallback. No video, generated photography, stock model, loop or pause icon. This is an illustrative interaction, not customer before/after evidence.
2. **Services.** Replace the boxed single-image feature panel with a continuous, draggable service rail. Nearby categories stay visible; selected artwork changes scale, depth and position through a spring. Name the section “Neyi yıkatalım?” and explain the selected service in ordinary Turkish. Remove checklists, orbit rings and ornamental badges. Keep address availability as a useful sentence.
3. **Pickup and return.** Preserve the one-bag narrative but remove the icon pins and checklist grammar. Use a fine track and large, simple step numbers. “Kapıyı açman yeter” explains the benefit. Viewport position advances the bag through pickup, care and return; direct selection gives the visitor control.
4. **App.** Keep one central phone with actual service artwork, remove floating reassurance tags and sparkle marks. The three text controls change its screen through a spring. Copy asks the concrete customer question “Siparişin hangi aşamada?” The download dialog carries store availability; it is not repeated as a badge in every section.
5. **Location.** The real map and real address are enough. Remove the location eyebrow, pin decorations and artificial reassurance. Center “Bakırköy’de bekleriz”, with one short invitation and three actions.

## Product

1. The app is the visual subject. Remove the eyebrow, floating claims, sparkle and platform badge. Refine the phone's movement and spacing; keep concise centered copy.
2. The order builder remains genuinely interactive. Remove summary icons and decorative numbering bubbles; use a continuous active indicator, fast state changes and direct choices. Do not imply a real order exists.
3. Tracking becomes a quiet segmented line with status names. Remove large generic icon circles. The actual shoe artwork and changing stage tell the story.
4. Service names, FAQ and CTA remain useful. Avoid extra slogans and decorative labels.

## Partners

1. Keep the operational panel as the subject, remove the hero eyebrow and chip claiming everything is under control. Scrolling moves the order through its columns; clicks select a column.
2. Replace the generic three-icon diagram with three numbered explanations of customer reach, order management and agreed capacity. A restrained rule connects the sequence. No invented earnings or volume promises.
3. Keep the centered application and understandable fields. Remove the application eyebrow and icon. Local-preview disclosure and validation remain because they communicate actual behavior.
4. Keep practical questions and one direct contact action. Remove decorative arrows.

## Shared interaction

Restore the earlier glass capsule using the existing real lens/filter implementation, with Home / Product / Partners and a simple download action. Keep its sliding active surface and mobile glass drawer. Text stays centered. No standalone pause UI: automatic entrance sequences end in under five seconds, offscreen work is suspended, and reduced motion skips them. Hover, drag, touch, keyboard and scrolling supply the ongoing response.

The journey, app screen, tracking and partner board now advance from actual viewport position rather than a timer. Direct selection takes control for the rest of that visit. Passive entrances remain bounded, while the scene itself responds to scroll, pointer, drag, touch and keyboard input.
